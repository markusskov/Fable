## en

> Fable 1.1 is a big, cozy update:
>
> • Story covers: every story can get its own painted cover, made entirely on your device
> • Summer Nights: a seasonal Fable+ story, in season on both halves of the world
> • iPad: Fable is now at home on iPad, in every orientation
> • Read aloud: stories narrate at a bedtime pace, and Personal Voice can read in the voice of someone who loves you
> • Story cards: share a finished story as one beautiful image
>
> Plus warmer typography in every language and small fixes. Everything still happens on your device. Nothing is ever sent or collected.
