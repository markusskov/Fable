## Where did my stories go after reinstall / new phone?

Fable keeps everything on your device only. There is no account and no
cloud copy, which is why the privacy label says Data Not Collected. The
cost of that promise is real: deleting the app deletes the library, and a
new phone starts fresh unless the story data came over in your device
transfer or iCloud device backup. I know that stings, and it is a
trade-off we chose openly rather than quietly collecting your family's
stories on a server.

## The story voice sounds robotic

iOS ships each language with a small compact voice preinstalled. Much
warmer voices are a free download: Settings, Accessibility, Spoken
Content, Voices, then your language, then download an Enhanced voice.
Fable switches to the best installed voice automatically. Fable can also
read in your own voice: record a Personal Voice in iOS Settings, then
allow it inside Fable's settings.

## Why did tonight's story come from the shelf, not the AI?

Fable prefers the on-device model, but bedtime never waits on it. If the
model is unavailable, still downloading, or writes something our safety
gate refuses, a hand-written story steps in silently. That is by design;
the settings screen explains which engine told each night's story.

## Subscription / refund questions

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us; we never see your
payment details. Manage or cancel: Settings app, your name, Subscriptions.
Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com. The free tier is yours forever: three
starter stories plus one new story a week, and every story you have
already told stays readable.

## A story contained something I did not like

Thank you for telling me, and I am sorry. Every story passes a safety
gate before display, and reports like yours are how the gate improves.
Please copy the story text (or screenshots) into a reply. The gate and
the model both run on your device, so nothing about your family leaves
your phone in this process either.

## Feature requests / language requests

Genuinely welcome, and more useful than you would guess for a one-family
company. Languages in particular: each one gets hand-written stories, not
machine translation, so they arrive one at a time and in the order
families ask.
