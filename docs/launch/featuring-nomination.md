# Apple featuring nomination (App Store Connect → Featuring nominations)

**When to submit:** with the v1.1 multilingual release, once 1.0 is approved
and released. Owner pastes; fields below map to the ASC nomination form.

**App:** Fable Bedtime — personalized bedtime stories

**What's new or noteworthy:**
Fable writes a new bedtime story for your child every night, entirely on
device with Apple Intelligence. No account, no server, no analytics: the
App Store privacy label is a single line, "Data Not Collected". Version 1.1
tells stories in seven languages (English, Norwegian, German, Spanish,
French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese), draws a cover for each story with
Image Playground, reads stories aloud with the family's own Personal Voice,
and brings a native iPad reading experience.

**Why it's a strong fit for featuring:**
- A flagship, family-friendly showcase of on-device Apple Intelligence:
  Foundation Models write the story, Image Playground paints the cover,
  Personal Voice reads it, and none of it leaves the phone.
- Every story a child sees passes a language-aware safety gate; if the
  model cannot deliver, a hand-written story shelf steps in silently. The
  app is engineered so bedtime never breaks.
- Calm by design: no streaks, no badges, no ads, no notifications. The
  paywall is honest and the free tier stays genuinely useful forever.
- Family Sharing, seven languages at launch, localized stores.

**Technologies used:** Foundation Models, Image Playground, Personal Voice
(AVSpeechSynthesizer), SwiftUI, SwiftData, StoreKit 2, String Catalogs.

**Suggested moments:** evening/bedtime editorial, Apple Intelligence
showcases, family collections, back-to-school evenings, App Store stories
about privacy-first design.
