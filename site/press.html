Fact sheet

App: Fable Bedtime, on the App Store for iPhone and iPad (iOS 26+)

What it does: writes your child a brand-new bedtime story every night: their name, their little companion, their favorite comfort object, woven into a calm tale that always lands softly in sleep. Painted covers, read-aloud narration, and the option to hear stories in a parent's own voice.

Privacy: no account, no server, no analytics. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. Story generation runs on the device with Apple Intelligence; when it is unavailable, a hand-written story library steps in.

Languages: English, Norwegian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese. Stories, not just menus: every language's story shelf is written by hand in that language.

Price: free forever (3 starter stories, then one new story a week). Fable+ for unlimited stories, story series, and a profile for every child. Family Sharing included; 7 day free trial on the yearly plan.

Founder: Markus Skov (Norway). Fable is built by a team of AI engineering agents under the founder's direction, and we are happy to talk about that too.

Contact: markusskov@gmail.com

The story for technology press

The interesting part is architectural: Fable is a generative AI consumer app with literally no backend. Foundation Models write the prose, Image Playground paints the covers, Personal Voice reads the story in a parent's own voice, and a deterministic, hand-written story engine sits underneath as the always-works floor. The safety design assumes the model is untrusted: every output crosses a language-aware content gate before a child sees it, and every failure falls silently to a calmer layer. There are no error states at bedtime, by design.

Two engineering deep-dives: An AI bedtime app with no server and The safety gate: shipping model output to a three-year-old.
